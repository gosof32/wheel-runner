WHEEL RUNNER

You run on the same wheel as Squeektoshi. His real speed on Squeekchain sets your speed. When he sleeps, the world goes dark.

  • Tap / Space / ↑ to jump. Tap again in the air to double-jump.
  • Collect seeds 🌻, dodge cats, mousetraps and cardboard.
  • Live RPM = game speed & score multiplier.
  • New Squeek block mined = Golden Wheel shield spawns.
  • Hamster asleep = Night Mode: slower, darker, seeds worth x2.

Call it before you run: will Squeektoshi still be running when your run ends?
